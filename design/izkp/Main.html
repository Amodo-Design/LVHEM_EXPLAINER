ZERO-KNOWLEDGE INFERENCE VERIFICATION

Proving which model answered, without showing the model.

The prover runs the model. The verifier checks it. The weights never leave the data centre.

1 Seal

The prover fingerprints its model and gives the fingerprint to the verifier.

2 Serve and record

Users are served as normal. A certifier on the link records every answer.

3 Sample

The verifier picks one recorded answer at random.

4 Prove

The prover shows that answer came from the sealed model.

5 Check

The verifier checks the proof against the fingerprint and the record.

STAYS INSIDE THE DATA CENTRE

The model weights.
Everything the model computes along the way.

WHAT THE VERIFIER HOLDS

A fingerprint of the model. A record of every answer.
A proof for each answer it sampled.
